**New finding from the B80 CSVs (not in either handoff): in practice, our score is our kill count.**
- `score − kills` averages **0.17**. In **164 of 192** games the two are equal, it is never negative, and the correlation is **r = 0.989**. House bots don't fight each other in practice (PR #22), so almost every mech that dies before us is one we killed. The rest are a few zone and drone deaths.
- So each extra kill is exactly +1 placement point, and +1/48 = +0.021 on the site. Seven commits landing on 182/48 means seven bots got the same kill total.
- **Our kill rate collapses late in the game**, while damage taken does not drop:

## c) Top 3 mid/late-game ideas, with evidence

1. **Late hunt: after 120 s, go looking for kills instead of holding.** Evidence: score is kill count (r = 0.989), and the kill rate halves after 120 s (3.1 → 1.5 → 0.66 per minute) while we still take about 2,800 damage. "hold" (30%) and "heal" (25%) are over half of all decisions (code audit H2). We win the gun trade 0.83 vs 0.18 accuracy. Shape: one flag, `LATE_HUNT_S = 120`. Once past it, with HP ≥ 60% and no LOS threat, the no-target branch walks to the nearest remembered or heard single house mech instead of holding. Measure first: in the 34 games alive at 120 s, count the ticks spent in hold/heal with no target, and how often a lone enemy was known within 150 m. **Overfit risk:** in the final, other bots kill each other, so survival is worth more there. Keep the flag so it can be set to 0 for the final if Grok wants.
2. **Win the supply drops (t = 70/140/210 s: +3 cubes and full HP).** Evidence: games with 6+ cubes score 8.76 vs about 3.5–4.3 below that. We bank only 457 cubes in 192 games (2.4 per game), and one drop is +3. A full heal at 95–235 s lands right where the kill rate collapses. PR #39 (on main) already flagged drops as the late-game heal. Measure first: does B80 reach the drop ring at all, how often, and is the ring contested? The per-game CSV has no drop column, so add `drop_claims` and `drop_arrivals` to the instrumentation. Build only if B80 claims fewer than about 1 drop per game that reaches 95 s.
3. **Lightning (H1, Doug's Claude): judge it on kills, not "lightning at end".** Evidence: gun+lightning ends score 2.67, below the 3.54 mean, but lightning+laser scores 7.38. "Lightning at end" (r ≈ 0.50 with site) looks like a proxy for "has two weapons and survived", not a cause. Lightning fires on only 6% of clear-target ticks (1,046 of 17,787), mostly at 45–90 m. The walk-in version lost on kills (646 → 627). Suggestion for Step A: report kills per minute after 60 s for lightning holders with vs without a laser, and make that the primary row instead of lightning at end. Cheap variant to test inside H1: when a laser holder also holds lightning, prefer an upgrade on the weapon that is actually firing. Upgrade picks today skew defensive (plating/armor 69 vs gun_rate+damage 21).
