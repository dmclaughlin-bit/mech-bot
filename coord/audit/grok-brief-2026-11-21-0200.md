## 2. What the score really measures
- Score = how many mechs die before us. House bots never fight each other, so **score ≈ our kills per match** (plus any house deaths to the zone). 3.50 ≈ 3.5 kills; the cutoff ≈ 4.9.
- Each kill adds a hunter, so every fight after the first is harder. What wins: more damage dealt per HP lost, and getting HP back between fights.
- History agrees. Gains came from damage and economy: B14 engage +0.34, B17 laser +0.42, B12/13 farm, B16 target, B19 no sneak. Losses came from hiding, cover, nav tweaks and aim changes.

## 3. Verified weak spots in the current bot (`brain.ts` at `badd7ad`)
1. **Stands still in every fight.** Hold, heal, and engage with a clear line all set `move = [0,0]` (about lines 665-668 and 710-716). The only combat movement is backing straight away during a retreat.
2. **Heal freezes the bot.** Below 90% HP (`HEAL_HP`), heal mode outranks loot, cubes, drops and farm, and the bot keeps firing. Every hit we land resets the 5 s regen timer, so it rarely heals with an enemy in view.
3. **Engage switches off once a second enemy sees us.** `alone()` gates both the finish rule and the TTK rule, and every kill adds a hunter. Offline, the TTK rule fired on 4 of 2,070 ticks.
4. **Enemy hit rates are guessed too high.** Gun 0.8/0.5 and laser 0.9, against measured house rates of 2-35% (gun beyond 100 m) and 15-27% (laser). Time-to-die is underestimated, so we leave fights we would win.
5. **Lightning is only wanted while holding just the gun,** and the gun is never swapped out with an enemy in view. Lightning does about 240 DPS inside 45 m; the laser does about 131 and the gun about 102.
6. **100 m gun gate** holds fire at sideways movers. B7 removed it and lost, but on the old 2.38 bot, before laser and engage. Re-test it.
7. **No pickups mid-fight.** After the 45 s laser rush, loot needs no enemy within 160 m.
8. **Never runs:** the mortar aim code (we never take a mortar). Leave it in; don't refactor before the lock.
