## Proof the report changes nothing
- **House mode:** `--trees` with `--death-report` on and off gave identical full traces on 96/96 seeds (base 3000), and identical CSV rows apart from the timing columns.
- **vs-15-champions mode:** the per-seed results don't reproduce even with the report off. Two identical runs differed on 5 of 8 seeds on a 4-core box. On the Mac, 41 of 384 seeds differed between two runs 10 minutes apart. 16 bot processes per game push the bot's wall-clock planning budget over. So only the vs-mode **set-level** counts should be trusted, not single seeds.

## Reading (for Grok to rank)
- **Retreat is where we die.** It is the bot's mode in 61-72% of our deaths in every set. Against house bots the top cause is "retreat, gun, 2+ saw us" at 40-120 s, with 62 and 55 deaths (726 and 648 placements lost). Against champions it is "retreat, gun, 1 saw us" in the first 40 s, with 41 and 35 deaths.
- **Retreating isn't breaking line of sight.** 85-89% of house deaths happen with 2+ enemies seeing us in our last 2 s, and only 0-17 deaths are unseen. So either the retreat starts too late (it starts at `retreatHp` 0.30), or it runs through open ground.
- **Most killing hits land on the front:** 146/190, 158/189, 146/179 and 148/174. That means we are usually still facing the attacker when we die.
- **Trees:** with trees on, a tree blocked some enemy's sightline in about half of the house deaths (90/190, 103/189). There is cover nearby that the retreat isn't using.
- Every new build can now be checked on whether "retreat × gun" goes down.
