# Mechanic synergy research (Sun Nov 22, 2026, ~3:35 AM ET)

Advisory research for Mech Builder, requested by Doug at 3:24 AM ET. Not a new system, agent, workflow, or mandatory stage. No new large sim batches were run. Numbers below come from files, traces, or comments that were actually read.

## 1. Does this consideration already exist?

**Partly, but not as a systematic check.** The existing process already requires evidence, one isolated change, and a `builds_on` citation. It does not ask the Builder, before choosing a change, to survey which champion mechanics already act in the same situations, or to record synergy or interference evidence in a dedicated field.

Quoted from the current files (main as read for this note):

- `coord/data/findings.md` lines 7–9: "New idea records must cite at least one finding ID below (field `builds_on`). Ideas with no finding behind them go to the bottom of the queue. […] One change per test (D3) still applies. A finding feeds the next single change; nothing gets bundled."
- `coord/LEARNING_STANDARD.md` lines 17–19 (idea record fields): "`builds_on`: finding IDs, or `NEW: <evidence>` […]. Also: `hypothesis`, `mechanism`, `activation`, `positives`, `negatives`, `confidence`, `reuse`, `outcome`, `disproof` (written BEFORE the build)." No `interacts_with` field. "Re-testing the same mechanism under a new name is not allowed."
- `coord/LEARNING_STANDARD.md` line 12: "Every build still starts from the current champion, with one change, and is kept only if its official score is strictly higher."
- `coord/BUILD_CHECKLIST.md` (through the 10:06 PM Step A section) covers record-first, one flag, opening/drops/sticking/early-death gates, and log-only Step A (10/192). It has no interaction or synergy check.
- Of 86 idea records in `coord/data/ideas/`, none had an `interacts_with` or `synergy` field. The closest existing practice is free-text in `mechanism`: P62 already wrote "Expected interaction with BACKAWAY_FAR_COVER_ON: none by construction (P60 fires only within 2 s of a hit, when P41's look is off; P60 changes legs only, P41 torso only)."

`builds_on` and "one change" are not the same as checking whether two existing mechanics help or hurt each other. That check was therefore added as a short, non-mandatory consideration at the end of `coord/BUILD_CHECKLIST.md`, plus an optional `interacts_with` field. `coord/WORKFLOW.md`, `coord/WORKFLOW_CHART.md`, and `coord/LEARNING_STANDARD.md` were not edited (the chart is locked; the standard's field list is a hard rule).

Same-tick and same-game interaction, from official wrap traces under `/workspace/tester/traces/trees-{d3c356a,afb6f26,c21d23b}-{2000,3000,4000,5000}` (384 games each) plus the matching CSVs in `/workspace/tester/runs/`. P62's wrapper records `earsT` when the label contains `(ears)`; far-cover ticks are ticks whose mode string is `retreat, far cover` (the P62 wrapper has no `farT` field, but the mode is recorded).

- **Same tick: 0 games.** No official P62 game has a look tick and a far-cover tick at the same timestamp or the same tick index. That matches the construction in P62.json: the look is off within 2 s of a hit, and the walk runs only within 2 s of a hit.
- **Same game: 16 of 384** (16 of 53 look games also have a walk; 112 P62 games have a walk). Minimum time gap 1.7 s (seed 2088), median 8.0 s; 1 game with gap < 2 s, 4 with gap < 5 s. In 13 of the 16, the look starts before the first walk.
- **Indirect coupling is real but mixed.** All 35 P62-vs-P60 CSV rows that changed any of place/score/death/kills/cubes had the look fire (expected, if the look is the only change). 15 of those 35 also had a far-cover walk in P60 or P62. The look changed whether a walk happened later in 8 games, all of them look games: 2 gained a walk (2088 unchanged place; 5075 6th→2nd, death 95.90→138.95 s, score 10→14) and 6 lost a walk (2037 worse 8th→10th; 3090 better 10th→8th; 3045, 4007, 4070, 5053 unchanged place/score). In the 5 games where a walk existed on both sides but its start time or tick count changed, the look had already fired, and the score split was 2 better / 3 worse (2079 worse, 2087 better, 3079 worse, 3003 better, 4038 worse). Among the 15 walk-involved changed games, 4 were better and 4 were worse. Among look-only changed games (no walk on either side), 9 were better and 4 were worse. Stall games 2021 (worse) and 5036 (better, 8th→2nd) had the look and **no** walk on either side.
- `coord/postmortems/P62.md` already records the unproven hypothesis: "EARS_LOOK's +0.09 on P48 does not stack on P60's back-away change, or it was site noise."

**Verdict:** there is **no evidence of same-tick interference**. There is evidence of occasional later-state coupling (the look can change whether or when a later walk happens), but that coupling does not have a consistent sign in the official sets. The site loss is small enough, and the local sign is inconsistent enough, that the fair reading is **no demonstrated interference; the site delta may be noise**. F19's "do not re-test EARS_LOOK on P60" still stands. This note does not propose another EARS_LOOK experiment.

## 3. Top evidence-supported mechanic-interaction opportunities

Each is one behavioral change on the current champion (P60, bot `afb6f26`). None is a bundle of two flags. P63, P64, and P65 are called out where they overlap; those three are already in flight and were not interrupted.

### Opportunity 1 — Align far-cover's "no closer" test with tree/building retreat (already in flight as P63)

- **Mechanics:** P60 `BACKAWAY_FAR_COVER_ON` and P48 `TREE_RETREAT_ON` / building `hideSpot`.
- **Interaction:** P60's fix-round hides the far spot only from enemies that hit us in the last 2 s, and its "no closer to the enemy" test uses that same short list. P48's tree retreat and `hideSpot` already apply the "no closer than we are now" rule to every enemy in view. The 47–70 m walk can therefore head toward a third enemy that is visible but has not shot yet.
- **Evidence:** P63.json Step 1, trees-on official sets, 116 P60 games with a far-cover walk: the condition "a far spot picked closer to an enemy in view (not one of the enemies the spot is checked against) than our current position" held in **4/4 collapses** (3017, 4001, 4043, 5024) vs **3/13 saves** (2058, 3085, 5040; in 5040 only at the last walk at t=118.2, not the saving walk at t=86.6; 2073 and 4093 were 0). Official screen (comment 7075869202): 5024 2nd death 143.25 s, 14 kills → 13th death 51.60 s, 3 kills (far cover at t=46.6); 4001 7th death 183.55 s, 9 kills → 13th death 124.70 s, 3 kills. Official traces of those four games all start the far-cover episode immediately after heal or heal-hiding (5024 heal 41.6–46.5 then far cover; 4001 heal 115.3–120.2; 4043 heal 82.7–87.6 then death at 88.55 s during a 0.8 s walk; 3017 heal then retreat then far cover). That heal-then-far pattern is common (81 of 116 walk games) and is mixed vs P48 (10 better, 12 worse, 59 same), so it is not a clean separator by itself. The third-enemy condition is the one with a 4/4 vs 3/13 split.
- **Activation frequency:** far-cover already fires in 53/192 and 63/192 official games. How often the safer search would change the chosen spot is P63's Step A (running; not completed in the record at the time of this note).
- **Expected effect:** fewer mid-game placement collapses after a walk; possible loss of some saves if the condition is not unique to collapses (it is also present in 51 games that did not collapse, per P63.json). Survival should rise in the collapse cell; kills and resources are uncertain (official 4000+5000 already lost kills 743→727 in P60 vs P48).
- **Regressions:** losing saves 2073 and 4093; a new 20 s pin; deaths before 40 s; a lower mean on both practice bases. P63.json already writes those as disproof.
- **Disproof:** already in P63.json: fails if it fires in < 10/192 on either official Step A set, or the saves (2073, 4093) are lost, or deaths < 40 s rise on either practice base, or the paired mean vs P60 is lower on both practice bases, or a new ≥20 s pin appears.
- **Already researched / tested?** This **is** P63 (`FAR_COVER_SAFE_ON`, branch in flight, Mech Builder). It has not been officially screened or site-tested. Do not start a duplicate.

### Opportunity 2 — Hold a valid far-cover latch; do not let tree, building, or plain back-away preempt it

- **Mechanics:** P60 far-cover walk vs P48 tree retreat, building-cover retreat (`retreat to cover`), and the plain back-away (`retreat`) fallback. P60 already latches the far spot *inside* the back-away branch, and drops the latch when the spot no longer hides us. The bot still leaves that branch as soon as a 1–3 s tree or building spot appears, or the mode falls back to plain back-away.
- **Interaction:** the intended 4–6 s walk (47–70 m) almost never happens. The three retreat modes interrupt each other on a sub-second scale, so far-cover mostly changes the first second of heading. P63.json reuse already noted this on a 142-episode slice ("92/142 P60 far-cover episodes are under 1 s and none gets within 10 m of its spot in far-cover mode: the walk mostly changes the first second of the retreat direction, not where we end up"). Official traces confirm and extend that measurement.
- **Evidence** (official P60 traces, 384 games, mode `retreat, far cover`): **116 games, 1220 ticks** (matches screens.csv 53 games / 518 ticks + 63 games / 702 ticks). Splitting an episode on any mode change: **192 episodes; 158/192 (82.3%) last < 1 s; median 0.4 s; mean 0.54 s; maximum 2.4 s; 0 episodes ≥ 3 s.** Short episodes travel a median **2.2 m**; the 34 episodes ≥ 1 s travel a median 19.2 m (max 41.7 m), still short of a 47–70 m spot. **38 episodes end the game**, all with death within 1.5 s of the last far-cover tick. Far-cover is within 1 s of tree retreat in **43** walk games, of building-cover in **34**, and of plain back-away in **62**. Union: **100/116** walk games (45/192 on 2000+3000, 55/192 on 4000+5000) have at least one of those preemptions; **69/116** (32/192 and 37/192) involve tree or building. Outcome vs P48 in the 77 high-flicker walk games (at least 3 retreat-mode switches with gap ≤ 1 s) is mixed: 11 better, 12 worse, 54 same — similar to all 116 walk games (13 better, 16 worse, 87 same). So the interrupts are frequent and the intended walk is not delivered, but interrupting is not yet shown to be what makes a game better or worse.
- **Activation frequency:** a "keep the latch across branch exits" change would have a place to fire in about 32/192 and 37/192 official games if limited to tree/building preemption, or 45/192 and 55/192 if plain back-away preemption is included. That is above the 10/192 Step A floor, but Step A still has to be measured as "flag would change the move," not merely "a preemption occurred."
- **Expected effect:** more walks that actually last several seconds and can reach cover, which is how the documented saves 2073 and 4093 were described (comment 7075869202). Placement and survival could rise if aborted walks are the flawed part of Refine B; they could fall if a brief tree or building spot is often the better exit. Kills and resources are uncertain. Official total retreat deaths did not fall under P60 (177→178 and 160=160), so a change that only shifts heading for 0.4 s is a plausible reason the official mean was weak.
- **Regressions:** walking farther into a third enemy (the P63 cell); new pins; abandoning a nearer tree or building that was actually safe; deaths < 40 s (the change should stay gated at t ≥ 40 s).
- **Disproof:** Step A fire-and-change < 10/192 on either official set, or the paired mean vs P60 is lower on both practice bases, or deaths < 40 s rise on either practice base, or a new ≥20 s pin appears, or saves 2073 and 4093 are lost.
- **Already researched / tested?** Not as its own idea. It is not P61 (that latched *building-cover* re-picks; outcome C, target cell unchanged, official screen VETO). It is not P63 (that filters *which* far spot is legal, and may cut walks rather than lengthen them). It is not P64 (that acts only when *no* far spot exists) or P65 (body-width test on building `hideSpot`). Partial overlap with P63 only in that both change far-cover legs. Check `coord/data/near_miss_ranking.md` retired list and `coord/data/ideas/`: no prior FAR_COVER commit/preempt idea. P33 (`HURT_RETREAT_FAR_ON`, retired, outcome C) kept an existing retreat going until the nearest enemy was ~120 m; that is a different mechanism and it barely fired.

### Opportunity 3 — The shared 40 s retreat gate leaves early back-away deaths untouched

- **Mechanics:** P60 far-cover and P48 tree retreat (and the building-cover retreat path) all inherit `TREE_RETREAT_MIN_T` (40 s).
- **Interaction:** a time gate chosen for tree retreat also suppresses far-cover, so pre-40 s back-away deaths cannot use the site-winning walk. That is missed coordination of when an existing mechanic may activate, not a new retreat type.
- **Evidence:** official P60 and P48 traces, retreat mode in the last 10 s before death, death_t < 40 s: **82 games on both bots**, unchanged by P60. Last retreat mode in those 82 is plain back-away in **67** (39 on 2000+3000, 28 on 4000+5000) and building-cover in **15**. Tree and far-cover never appear as the last mode before a sub-40 s death, as required by the gate. P60.json Step A on P48, counting back-away decisions that the far search did *not* change, already lists `t<40` as the largest skip bucket: **2691** decisions on 2000+3000 and **2019** on 4000+5000, ahead of "no far spot" (1394 and 1620). P60.json negatives: "'no far spot' or t < 40 s explain most of the rest" of the 79 target-cell deaths. Official P60 vs P48 deaths < 40 s were 48=48 and 38=38 (`screens.csv`).
- **Activation frequency:** 67 official games end in a plain back-away death before 40 s. That is the size of the cell, not a Step A fire-and-change count. **Whether a 4–6 s far spot actually exists in those pre-40 s decisions was not measured** (Step A only searched t ≥ 40 s). A log-only Step A on P60, with the 40 s gate lifted in the logger only, is required before building. If that run is under 10/192 on either set, the idea retires without a build.
- **Expected effect:** some of those 67 early back-away deaths could reach cover, which would raise survival and placement in the early-death cell. The risk is the opposite: changing pre-40 s movement can *raise* deaths before 40 s, which blocks handoff (BUILD_CHECKLIST item 5). Kills and resources are uncertain. The behavior must still stay off in the opening (t < 10 s, item 2).
- **Regressions:** higher deaths before 40 s; opening activations; drop-run conflicts (P33 died of a mid-phase drop gate); new pins; interference with laser rush, which is still common before 40 s.
- **Disproof:** Step A fire-and-change < 10/192 on either official set, or deaths < 40 s rise on either practice base, or 0 activations between 10 s and 40 s that actually change the move, or the paired mean vs P60 is lower on both practice bases, or a new ≥20 s pin appears.
- **Already researched / tested?** Not this gate on far-cover. P32 (fight fade) failed checklist item 5 (deaths < 40 s 33→35) and was retired. P33 (hurt retreat far) fired 0/96 on base 0 because every mid-phase retreat end had a drop live, and was retired. P64 is the "no far spot" leftover of the *t ≥ 40 s* cell (~56 of 79 in F16), not the t < 40 s cell. P63 and P65 stay gated at 40 s. No retired idea in `near_miss_ranking.md` is "far-cover before 40 s."

No fourth idea is listed. Other leads were weaker or already assigned: heal-hiding / ambush still using zero-width `hideSpot` is P65 reuse #2 (one practice pin, seed 46, 22 s, 0 P65 flag ticks) and should wait for the P65 screen; a knee-height walk line is a fix to one test, not an interaction; re-testing EARS_LOOK, even with a narrower gate, is blocked by F19 and is not supported by a consistent interference sign.

## 4. Which opportunity deserves the next experiment?

**P63 is already the right next experiment** for the strongest interaction (opportunity 1). Mech Builder is running it; this note does not change that brief or start a fourth lane.

The first *new* experiment after the current lanes (P63, P64, P65) should be **opportunity 2** (hold the far-cover latch across tree/building/plain preemption), not opportunity 3 and not another EARS_LOOK test. Reason: official traces show the mechanic that just won on the site almost never does the 4–6 s walk it was designed to do (max episode 2.4 s, 82% under 1 s, 100 of 116 walk games preempted within 1 s). That is a direct coordination bug between existing champion retreat modes, it is not covered by P63/P64/P65, and a Step A is likely to clear the 10/192 floor. Opportunity 3 is a real cell (67 official early back-away deaths) but its fire rate is unmeasured and it is the higher-risk change against the deaths-before-40 s gate. Run a log-only Step A for opportunity 3 only if a lane is free and opportunity 2 has already been handed off or retired.
